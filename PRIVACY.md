# Privacy policy: AI Foundations Website Builder

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the AI Foundations Website Builder plugin for Claude Code (the "plugin"). The plugin is a set of instructions and small scripts that run on your own computer and in accounts you own. AI Foundations runs no server for this plugin and receives no data from it.

## What AI Foundations collects

Nothing. The plugin has no telemetry, no analytics and no connection to AI Foundations. We never receive your business information, your website content, your leads or your conversations with Claude.

## What the plugin handles, and where it goes

Everything stays on your computer or in accounts you control. With your approval at each step, the plugin may:

- **Write files on your computer:** your business details, page content, keyword research and lead magnets, all in your own project folder.
- **Read files you choose to give it,** such as a customer export used to find the areas you serve. These are read on your computer and are never published or sent anywhere.
- **Publish your website** to Cloudflare Pages (or another host you choose), through your own account and its own sign-in.
- **Store your website's form leads** (for example name, email, phone and message), if you set up the optional Lead Desk. They are kept in a database in your own Cloudflare account, and only you and the people you invite can see them. You can export or delete any lead at any time.
- **Send lead alerts** only to destinations you enter on the Lead Desk Settings page: your email addresses (through Cloudflare Email Routing), a webhook link you paste in (for example Slack, Discord, Google Chat, Microsoft Teams, Zapier or Make), or an ntfy phone topic.
- **Count website visits** for conversion rates, if the Lead Desk helper script is on your site. It uses no cookies and stores no IP addresses or anything else that identifies a visitor.
- **Fetch public information:** your own site's public pages, Google PageSpeed Insights results for them, public search results and competitor pages for keyword research, and `api.indexnow.org` to notify Bing about new pages.

## Credentials

The plugin never asks for, reads or stores passwords, API keys or tokens. Services that need a login are connected by you, through their own sign-in pages or through connectors you add to Claude. Lead Desk passwords and alert links are typed by you into your own Lead Desk pages. Lead Desk passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

## How long data is kept

AI Foundations keeps no data, because it receives none. Data in your project folder and your Cloudflare account is kept for as long as you choose, and you can delete it at any time.

## Third-party services

When you use Cloudflare, Google, Bing, an email or chat tool, or any other service through the plugin, that service's own privacy policy applies to the data you send it.

## Your website's visitors

If your website collects leads, you are responsible for telling your visitors what you collect and why. The plugin helps you add a privacy section to your site that says this.

## Children

The plugin is not intended for people under 18.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this repository, and the date at the top is updated. For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/cartcodes/claude-website-builder/issues.
